# Data availability

The public replication package is
<https://github.com/HDPark95/loop-engineering-lab> under the MIT License.

It contains:

- the full apparatus and analysis code;
- the revised preregistration draft and amendment log;
- the original raw pilot cycle logs under `logs/`;
- aggregate-only AIDev feasibility outputs under `results/aidev_pilot/`;
- held-out-oracle apparatus fixtures for S1 and S3;
- a 2x2 apparatus smoke result explicitly marked as non-research evidence.

The external AIDev parquet tables are not redistributed. They are available
under CC BY 4.0 from <https://huggingface.co/datasets/hao-li/AIDev>. The exact
dataset revision, file checksums, deterministic sample rule, and downloader are
included so the field pilot can be reproduced byte for byte.

The package does not yet have a software DOI. DOI publication is intentionally
deferred until the confirmatory artifact is frozen.
